NOICE ?

One minute of audio. Three words. Uploaded by people who were actually there.

Cities are constantly documented through images, but sound is just as important in shaping how a place feels. Noise levels, conversations, traffic, music leaking from storefronts, train delays, construction, and moments of silence all contribute to a city's atmosphere in ways visuals alone cannot fully capture.

NOICE is a crowdsourced archive of New York City's sonic landscape, collecting everyday recordings from different neighborhoods, times, and experiences to create a more immediate and unfiltered portrait of life in the city. Each submission acts as a small record of presence, capturing how New York changes across different environments, communities, and moments in time.
